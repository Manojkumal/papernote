#### Beyond Self-Talk: A Communication-Centric Survey of LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems
#### note
This paper presents a comprehensive survey of Large Language Model-based Multi-Agent Systems (LLM-MAS) through a communication-centric lens, shifting the focus from application domains to the mechanics of agent interaction
. The authors propose a structured framework that distinguishes between system-level communication (macro organization) and system-internal communication (micro dynamics)
. By analyzing recent literature, the survey identifies how structured architectures, goals, and standardized protocols—such as MCP, A2A, and ANP—enable agents to transcend individual "self-talk" and achieve collective intelligence
.
# comment Advantages of the Communication-Centric Perspective:
* Unified Taxonomy: The paper organizes agent organization into five canonical architectures: Flat, Hierarchical, Team, Society, and Hybrid, providing a clear roadmap for designing systems based on task complexity
.
* Strategic Dynamics: It breaks down internal communication strategies (e.g., One-by-One vs. Simultaneous-Talk) and paradigms (e.g., Speech Act vs. Blackboard), offering technical insights into how message flow affects system latency and accuracy
.
* Security and Robustness: The survey highlights the critical need for cryptographically grounded protocols to defend against "man-in-the-middle" and data-tampering attacks as agents move into high-stakes domains
.
* Interoperability Solutions: By comparing emerging protocols like the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and Agent Network Protocol (ANP), the paper identifies a path toward a "consensus-driven standard" similar to HTTPS for the internet
.
#### highlight
* "LLM-MAS combine the linguistic flexibility of LLM agents with the coordination principles of MAS, thereby dissolving the boundary between natural-language dialogue and agent communication."
* "A unified, standardized communication protocol... would provide foundational interoperability, enhance security, simplify integration, and significantly reduce complexity."
* "Communication with self refers to internal dialogues... crucial for agents to update their internal state and manage cognitive resources."
* "Existing leaderboards... rarely capture system-level properties, including coordination efficiency, communication bandwidth, and latency."
* "Future research should focus on improving the fusion of multimodal data and designing stronger agents... for handling multimodal content."
