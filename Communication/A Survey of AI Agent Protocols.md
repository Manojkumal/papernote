A Survey of AI Agent Protocols

#### note
This paper addresses the lack of standardized protocols for Large Language Model (LLM) agents, which currently hinders their ability to communicate with external tools, data sources, or each other. The authors propose a systematic two-dimensional classification to organize existing protocols: differentiating between context-oriented vs. inter-agent protocols, and general-purpose vs. domain-specific protocols. The goal is to create a unified communication framework that enables collective intelligence and allows agents to tackle complex, real-world tasks more effectively.

#### comment
**Advantages of Standardized Protocols:**
* Interoperability: Allows agents and tools to interact smoothly, encouraging collaboration rather than operating in silos.
* Scalability: Solves the issue where a lack of standards makes it difficult for agent systems to scale effectively.
* Performance Insight: The paper provides a comparative analysis across critical dimensions including security, scalability, and latency.

**Key Findings and Future Directions:**
Next-generation protocols must prioritize adaptability, privacy preservation, and group-based interaction.
* There is a clear trend toward layered architectures and infrastructures designed specifically for collective intelligence.
* The survey serves as a practical reference for both researchers and engineers to design or integrate robust communication infrastructures for intelligent agents.
