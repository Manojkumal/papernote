Here is the simple, step-by-step process of how this system works:
Step 1: Start with "Golden Truths." Before the AI begins its main job, it is trained on a "golden standard." This is a collection of information that humans have checked and verified to be 100% correct. This serves as the AI's foundation of "common sense" or "facts".
Step 2: Question New Information. When the AI is given new data to learn from, it doesn't just accept it immediately. It compares the new information against its "golden standard" to see if it makes sense. For example, if the AI knows that "1 + 1 = 2" and a new piece of data tells it "1 + 1 = 3," the AI will flag this as suspicious.
Step 3: Assign a "Trust Score." Every piece of new information is given a credibility score. If the data matches what the AI already knows to be true, it gets a high score. If the data looks messy, wrong, or "polluted," it gets a low score.
Step 4: Learn Selectively. The AI uses these scores to decide how to update its "brain." If a piece of information has a high trust score, the AI learns from it normally. If the score is low, the AI "slows down" its learning or ignores that data entirely so it doesn't pick up bad habits or incorrect fact
